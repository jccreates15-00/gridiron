How to play

You get 10 picks to assemble a 15-man starting roster. Each round, three players flash on the board — choose one. The five jobs you can't fill get a generic league-average starter. Your finished roster is graded by a multi-factor model into a record out of a possible 17-0 regular season + 4 playoff wins = 21-0 — then you watch that season play out against real historical teams, with the playoffs pitting you against the all-time greats.

10
Ten picks, fifteen slots.
Position caps: QB1 · RB1 · WR2 · TE1 · OL2 · DL2 · LB2 · CB2 · S2. You can't fill them all — decide which five spots to punt to an average starter.
2
Two re-spins. For the entire game.
Hate all three cards? Re-spin the board — but you only get two re-spins total across all ten rounds. Spend them wisely.
★
Legends and current stars.
Legends are historic seasons with real stats — rarer on the board, and they hit harder. Current players are rated for 2026 by archetype, with badges for last season's award winners, All-Pros and rookies. Read the attributes, not just the OVR.
📅
Daily Draft.
One locked-in draft per day. Your boards are random and unique to you, and they're saved as you go, so refreshing won't reroll them. Share your result and compare records with friends. Free Play is unlimited and fully random every time.
⚖
Balance wins titles.
A great offense with no defense can win games but gets exposed in January. The deep playoff rounds demand a roster that's strong on both sides. 21-0 is rare by design.

Current-player ratings reflect the 2026 season; legend ratings and stats reflect the labeled season. All ratings are gameplay approximations for entertainment only. Not affiliated with the NFL or any team or player.
